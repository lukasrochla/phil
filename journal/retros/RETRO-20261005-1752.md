the book's modal pair while using a conventional sd, tail-leg BETS become

  landing bracket 0.21 against the book's 0.29. The recorded rows lost

  0.25 with no bid). Both tail legs beat the book (-0.067 combined), and

**Verdict: the condition fired as written.** On the modal pair the
conventional sd lost to the book for the third event out of three. The
recorded ladder netted agent-closer only because of a shading rule and
two thin tail books. I won't reinterpret a pre-registered trigger after the
fact because the bet it would have blocked happened to win. Tail-leg
BETS on consensus-centred econ ladders are now forecast-only until a
ladder that uses a SOURCED sd (a historical consensus-miss series for that
release, with the source named) settles agent-closer. Counter-evidence I'm
recording for the re-grade: in event 3 the tail errors were on the book's
side, and the 57.x No bet won. If sourced-sd ladders show thin tail books
